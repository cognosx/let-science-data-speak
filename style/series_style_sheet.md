# Let Science Data Speak — series style sheet

One page for authors, editors and reviewers of all three books. When in doubt, follow the *Canadian Oxford Dictionary* and *Editing Canadian English*.

## Spelling (Canadian)

| Use | Not | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| organize, organization, standardize, normalize, summarize, recognize, prioritize, randomized | organise, organisation, standardise… | -ize in all verb forms and their nouns |
| analyze, analyzed, analyzing | analyse… | the noun plural stays *analyses* |
| centre, centres | center | except in proper names and quoted titles (e.g. *National Center for Biotechnology Information*) |
| colour, behaviour, favour, labour | color, behavior… | -our |
| licence (noun), license (verb) | | "a CC BY licence"; "you may license your data" |
| practice (noun), practise (verb) | | the "Practise" boxes are verbs |
| program, programs | programme | including research and vaccine programs; keep *programme* only in proper names |
| modelling, travelled, catalogue | | Canadian keeps the double *l* and *-ogue* |

Never change spelling inside quoted titles, reference lists, search strings or proper names. A search string may need both spellings (e.g. `immunization OR immunisation`).

## Citations in the text

- Author–date, with "and colleagues" for three or more authors: (Peters and Chin-Yee, 2025); (Lau and Golder, 2025); (Wang and colleagues, 2023).
- Two works by the same first author in one year: 2007a, 2007b, with the letters in the reference list too.
- Every citation has an entry in that chapter's reference list, and every entry is cited or named in that chapter.

## Reference lists

- Vancouver-style entries: authors (up to six, then "et al."), title, *Journal*, year;volume(issue):pages, doi:.
- Web sources: full https address and "(accessed day month year)".
- Preprints: server and identifier (arXiv:2505.07155; *medRxiv*).

## Numbers, dates and units

- Numerals for 10 and above and for all measurements and percentages (5%, 12,301 records).
- Thin space not used; comma as the thousands separator (44,500).
- Dates: 17 November 2026. Periods: 2000–2009 (en dash, no spaces).
- "per cent" is written as % in text and tables.

## Voice and plain English

- Average sentence under 20 words; split sentences over about 35 words.
- Plain words for readers who use English as a second language.
- "I" only for the author's own experience; never invented.
- At most one short "X, not Y" contrast per section.

## Fixed series terms

Essentials · What AI does / what you decide · Practise · Key takeaways · Trap (T01–T49) · evidence pack · claim check · portfolio plan · trust level (Green, Amber, Red).
