# Chapter 3 — AI screening: published evidence

Evidence checked in November 2026. These numbers will age; update this file as new studies appear. The book keeps only the summary.

| Study | What was tested | Result |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Meta-analysis of 18 studies, 2026 (preprint) | LLM screening of titles and abstracts | Pooled sensitivity 0.92 (95% CI 0.81–0.96), specificity 0.94 (0.90–0.97) |
| Dai and colleagues, 2025 | Two LLMs on thoracic surgery reviews | Sensitivity 0.73 at first; 0.98 only after the prompt was revised by looking at errors |
| otto-SR, 2025 (preprint) | An AI agent on 32,357 citations from five reviews | 96.7% sensitivity, against 81.7% for human reviewers |
| López-Pineda and colleagues, 2025 (1,081 records) | GPT-4o mini and Llama 3 | Best configurations: sensitivity 56.2% and 77.5% |
| Dennstädt and colleagues, 2024 (ten datasets) | Four open models | Sensitivity often above 94%, but specificity as low as 19% |
| Homiar and colleagues, 2025 (a living review) | GPT-4o with refined prompts | All studies finally included were found; specificity moderate |
| Vendor report (Elicit, 2025) | Its own screening model | 97% sensitivity, 93% specificity on its own benchmark (a vendor figure) |
