#### the analyst: from species to clinical meaning

For the analyst, "lab to patient" means asking which new respiratory species have been reported in infections. He links each species name to case reports in PubMed, by name and by synonym, because species are often renamed after description. A species with no case report is not proof of harmlessness; it may simply be hard to identify in routine laboratories.

#### How the analyst did it

**The no-code path.** On ClinicalTrials.gov, the analyst searched the intervention field for "bacteriophage OR phage OR phages" and downloaded the results as a CSV file: 125 trials. She added a column for "phage as a whole word in title or intervention" (88 trials), and read the other 37 one by one. Many were false hits, such as studies on air pollution or cancer biosensors, but some were real phage products named only by code. For the 20 completed phage trials, she searched PubMed for each registration number, in the abstract and in the databank field (`NCT0XXXXXXX[si]`), and noted the hits: 6 trials had a paper. Then she searches by trial name and sponsor for the other 14, before writing anything about publication.

**The code path.**

```python
import requests, pandas as pd
rows, token = [], None
while True:
    p = {"query.intr": "bacteriophage OR phage OR phages", "pageSize": 200, "format": "json"}
    if token: p["pageToken"] = token
    r = requests.get("https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies", params=p).json()
    rows += [s["protocolSection"]["identificationModule"]["nctId"] for s in r["studies"]]
    token = r.get("nextPageToken")
    if not token: break
```

Each completed trial's number then goes into a PubMed search: `f"{nct}[si] OR {nct}[tiab]"`.

**What the analyst reports.** "Of 20 completed registered phage trials, 6 had a PubMed paper that cites their registration number, and 3 had posted results; we searched the remaining 14 by name and sponsor." And beside it: "Much phage therapy happens under compassionate use and is reported as case reports, which registries do not capture."
