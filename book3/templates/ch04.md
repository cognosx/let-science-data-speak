## Template: objectives and gaps sheet (one page)

| Section | Content |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The field in brief | Three sentences, with key numbers and the date of your search |
| Three tested gaps | Gap, kind, evidence it is real, why it matters |
| Research question | One sentence |
| Objectives | Two to four, specific and time-bound |
| Why now | One or two sentences |
| Why us | One or two sentences |
| Key references | Five to ten from the reading list |

## Template: design dossier

| Section | Content |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Design question | One sentence |
| Evidence base | Corpus, search date, number of papers, extraction method and accuracy |
| What the field has tried | Main patterns, with one or two tables |
| What has not been tried | Candidate options, with how you tested that they are untried |
| Proposed design | Conditions or design chosen, with reasons from the evidence |
| Risks and alternatives | What could fail, and the next options |
| Sharing plan | Where the knowledge table and results will be deposited |
