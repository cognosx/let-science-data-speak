## Template: press summary

| Section | Content |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Headline | True, specific, not exaggerated |
| Summary | One or two sentences with the key number |
| Why it matters | Two or three sentences |
| How we know | Data, method, dates in two sentences |
| What it does not show | One or two sentences |
| Quote | One named researcher |
| Contacts | Names, roles, languages, time zones |
| Links | Paper or preprint; dataset; dashboard; images with credit |
