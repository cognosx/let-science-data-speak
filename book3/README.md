# Book 3 — Science That Serves: companion files

Companion files for *Science That Serves: Turning Research Findings into Papers, Policy Briefs, Datasets and Public Impact* (Let Science Data Speak, Book 3), by Maxime Descartes Mbogning Fonkou.

The book keeps the release pipeline, the principles and the checks. Templates, exercises and lists that change often live here, with dates.

| File or folder | What it holds | Book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `templates/` | Templates for every output: portfolio plan, objectives sheet, article outline, policy brief, competitive landscape, data story, dashboard and others | Appendix A, Chapters 1–10 |
| `exercises/` | A short exercise for each chapter (moved from the printed chapters) | Chapters 1–10 |
| `keeping_it_alive/` | The full tool table, living-report template and two-day workshop plan from the former Chapters 10 and 11, merged in the book into Chapter 10 | Chapter 10 |
| `where_to_publish_and_drafting_briefs.md` | Where to publish and deposit each output, and the drafting briefs for AI, checked November 2026 (former Appendices D and E) | Appendices D and E |

Journal and funder rules on AI change often. Check each one at its source, and note the date you checked.
