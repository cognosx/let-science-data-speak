*Part A, gaps and objectives (2–3 hours)*

1. Summarise your field in three sentences from your landscape.
2. List five candidate gaps and name their kind.
3. Run the eight tests on the two most promising.
4. Write one question and three objectives for the strongest.
5. Choose 30 papers for an annotated reading list.
6. Draft the five paragraphs of a grant state-of-the-art section.

*Part B, better experiments (3–4 hours)*

1. Write one design decision you face in your current work.
2. List the information you need from the literature.
3. Build a small corpus of 50 to 100 relevant papers.
4. Extract five columns into a knowledge table, with source sentences.
5. Build one cross-table and look for patterns and empty cells.
6. Write a one-page design dossier.
