# Chapter 10 — Keeping It Alive and Passing It On: exercises

## Part A — Dashboards and living reports

1. Name two users and write their three most important questions.
2. Choose one indicator per question and write its indicator card.
3. Build a one-page dashboard in a simple tool, with date, sources and limits.
4. Write the update routine and the change log template.

## Part B — Teaching (formerly Chapter 11)

1. Write three learning outcomes for a half-day session on one method.
2. Build a teaching subset of 200 records with known answers.
3. Plan three sessions with short explanations and long practice.
4. Write five quiz questions with answers.
5. Run the session with three colleagues and collect feedback.
