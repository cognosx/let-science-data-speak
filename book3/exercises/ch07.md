*Part A, briefs and reports (2–3 hours)*

1. Identify one real decision your analysis could inform, and its decision-maker.
2. Write three key messages for that reader.
3. Draft a two-page brief with options and one chart.
4. Ask someone who works in policy to read it and tell you what is missing.
5. From your entity columns, draw a small evidence gap map.

*Part B, portfolio reviews and priorities (2–3 hours)*

1. Choose one funder in your landscape.
2. List its grants or acknowledged papers, and link them to outputs.
3. Compare its portfolio by topic and stage with the whole field.
4. Write one page on what the comparison shows and what it cannot show.
5. List three candidate priorities and the evidence for each.
