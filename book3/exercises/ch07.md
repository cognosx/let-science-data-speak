1. Agree the question and the horizon with one real reader, for example a programme manager: "What could research on our topic look like in ten years?"
2. List five candidate drivers from your own analysis, each with one piece of evidence and its date.
3. Ask three to six people to rate each driver on importance and uncertainty. Report the spread, not only the average.
4. Cross the two drivers rated most important and most uncertain, and name the four futures.
5. Write one scenario in full (about 120 words) from your evidence pack only, with what it means for the reader and two indicators with today's values.
6. Run the claim check on the scenario: remove "will", "inevitable" and "most likely" unless the evidence supports them.
