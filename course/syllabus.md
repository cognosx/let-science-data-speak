# Syllabus — chapters of the three books, week by week

This maps the weekly French course *Faire parler les données de la science : de zéro aux livrables* (with English subtitles) to the chapters of the *Let Science Data Speak* series. Use it to follow the course with the books, or to build your own course. A 12-week seminar on Book 1 alone is in `book1/course_outline.md`.

Book 1: *Science as Data*. Book 2: *Science You Can Check*. Book 3: *Science That Serves*.

## Season 1 — from zero to first outputs (26 weeks, March–August 2027)

| Weeks | Module | Read | Make |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | 0. Getting started | Book 1, Ch 1–4 and 16 | Your question and your sources |
| 4–7 | 1. Set up and code just enough | Book 2, Appendix D and Ch 1 | A working workspace; one AI step measured |
| 8–14 | 2. Build your corpus | Book 1, Ch 5; Book 2, Ch 2–4; Book 3, Ch 5 | A clean, enriched dataset with a DOI (Output 1) |
| 15–20 | 3. First analyses | Book 2, Ch 6–9; Book 1, Ch 9–10 | Trends, shares with intervals, networks, topics, links to trials and funding |
| 21–26 | 4. First outputs | Book 3, Ch 2, 6 and 10 | A policy brief (Output 2) and a dashboard (Output 3) |

## Season 2 — deeper analyses and a first paper (25 weeks, September 2027–February 2028)

| Weeks | Module | Read | Make |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 9–16 | 6. Patents, trials, policy | Book 2, Ch 9 and 14; Book 1, Ch 8, 10 and 12 | Results linked to patents, trials and policy |
| 17–25 | 7. From landscape to paper | Book 3, Ch 2 and 4; Book 2, Ch 10 | A paper-quality analysis, ready to submit (Output 4) |

## Season 3 — automate, measure differently, build a career (35 weeks, March–November 2028)

| Weeks | Module | Read | Make |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–12 | 8. Pipelines and agents | Book 2, Ch 11–12 | A documented pipeline |
| 13–22 | 9. Frontier measures | Book 2, Ch 13 | One indicator, tested in two versions |
| 23–35 | 10. Careers, funding effects and foresight | Book 2, Ch 14–15; Book 3, Ch 7 and 11 | An open state-of-the-field report (Output 5) and your own 12-month plan |

Every chapter of Books 1 and 2 ends with a "Practise" box; every chapter of Book 3 points to its exercise in `book3/exercises/`.
